Information Cards Collection

Welcome to the Information Cards webpage. This page displays different types of cards, including Student, Employee, Product, Movie, and Company details.

🎓 Student Card

A student card contains important academic information about a student. It includes the student's name, roll number, department, college, and email address. Student cards are commonly used in schools and colleges for identification, attendance, examinations, and library access.

👨‍💼 Employee Card

An employee card provides professional information about an employee. It contains details such as employee ID, designation, department, and years of experience. Organizations use employee cards for identification, office access, and maintaining employee records.

📦 Product Card

A product card displays information about a product available for customers. It includes the product name, brand, price, category, and stock availability. Product cards help customers understand product features and make better purchasing decisions.

🎬 Movie Card

A movie card presents details about a film, including its title, hero, director, release year, and genre. Movie cards help viewers learn about popular films before watching them and provide quick information about the cast and story category.

🏢 Company Card

A company card provides an overview of a business organization. It includes the company name, founder, year of establishment, headquarters, and industry. Company cards are useful for learning about different organizations and the services they provide.
