My Childhood Kabaddi Memories...

My School Days (2007-2009)

During the years 2007 and 2008, when I was studying in the 3rd and 4th standards, one of the happiest parts of my life was playing Kabaddi.
Every day after school, I would rush to the playground near my home and spend nearly five hours playing with my friends.
Kabaddi was not just a game for me—it was my passion and the place where I felt truly alive.

That Never Stopped Me...

Almost every day, I came home with small scratches, bruises, and injuries on my hands and legs.
Sometimes I had two, four, or even six small wounds after a single day of playing.
Even though they caused pain, I never complained or stopped playing because my love for Kabaddi was much stronger than the pain.
