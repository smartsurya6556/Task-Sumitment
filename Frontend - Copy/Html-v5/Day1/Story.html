My Childhood Kabaddi Memories...

My School Days (2007-2009)

During the years 2007 and 2008, when I was studying in the 3rd and 4th standards, one of the happiest parts of my life was playing Kabaddi. Every day after school, I would rush to the playground near my home and spend nearly five hours playing with my friends. Kabaddi was not just a game for me—it was my passion and the place where I felt truly alive.

I Love Kabaddi

I enjoyed playing as both a raider and a defender. I loved attacking, escaping from defenders, tackling opponents, and helping my team win. Every match taught me courage, teamwork, confidence, and determination. Playing Kabaddi filled my heart with excitement, and I always looked forward to the next game.

The Small Injuries

That Never Stopped Me Almost every day, I came home with small scratches, bruises, and injuries on my hands and legs. Sometimes I had two, four, or even six small wounds after a single day of playing. Even though they caused pain, I never complained or stopped playing because my love for Kabaddi was much stronger than the pain.

Looking back now, those small injuries were not something I hated. They became symbols of my passion, hard work, and unforgettable childhood memories. Every scratch reminded me that I had played with all my heart.

Remembering Those Beautiful Days

Now, in 2026, whenever those memories return to my mind, I smile. I remember the playground, my friends, the excitement of every raid, every tackle, and every moment spent running with endless energy. Those days were simple, joyful, and full of life. They remain some of the most precious memories of my childhood.

My Prayer

Dear God and Universe,

Thank You for giving me such beautiful childhood memories. Whenever I remember those Kabaddi days, my heart fills with happiness and gratitude. If it is Your will, I pray that I may once again experience the same joy, energy, friendship, and passion that I felt during those unforgettable days.

Even if I cannot relive those exact moments, please let their spirit continue to inspire me throughout my life. May I always carry the courage, determination, strength, and happiness that Kabaddi gave me as a child.

Thank You for every memory, every lesson, and every blessing.

Forever Grateful...
