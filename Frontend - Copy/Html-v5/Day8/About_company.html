About the Company


Zoho Corporation is an Indian multinational technology company that develops cloud-based software for businesses. It was founded in 1996 and is well known for creating affordable and secure business applications. Zoho provides more than 55+ software products that help organizations manage sales, finance, human resources, customer relationships, email, collaboration, and business operations. The company focuses on customer privacy, innovation, and self-funded growth. Unlike many technology companies, Zoho has grown without relying on external investors and serves millions of users worldwide.
