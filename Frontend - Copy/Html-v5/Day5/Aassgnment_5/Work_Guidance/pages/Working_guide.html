Delivery Partner Working Guide


Daily Work Routine

A delivery partner should follow a daily routine to provide fast and reliable service. Starting the day with proper preparation helps avoid delays and improves customer satisfaction.

  • 🌅 Wake up and prepare for work.
  • 📱 Charge your mobile phone.
  • 🛵 Check your bike and fuel level.
  • 🪖 Wear your helmet and safety gear.
  • 📍 Turn on GPS and mobile data.
  • 🔐 Login to the delivery partner app.
  • ✅ Start accepting delivery requests.

How to Manage Orders

Managing orders correctly helps complete deliveries quickly. Always check the order details before leaving the restaurant.

Order Management Tips

  • 📲 Accept nearby orders.
  • 🍔 Check the restaurant location
  • 🧾 Verify the food items.
  • 📦 Keep the food package secure.
  • 🚴 Start delivery immediately.
  • 📍 Follow GPS directions.
  • ✅ Confirm delivery after handing over the order.

Managing Your Time

Good time management helps complete more deliveries and reduces waiting time.

  • ⏰ Start work on time.
  • 📍 Stay near busy restaurants.
  • 🍽️ Work during lunch and dinner hours.
  • 🚦 Avoid heavy traffic routes when possible.
  • 📱 Keep the delivery app online.
  • 🚴 Complete one order before accepting another (if required by the platform).

End of the Day

After Completing Work


At the end of your shift, review your completed deliveries and prepare for the next working day.


Checklist

  • ✅ Complete all active deliveries.
  • 📊 Check your earnings in the app.
  • 🔋 Charge your phone.
  • ⛽ Refill fuel if needed.
  • 🛵 Clean your delivery bag.
  • 😴 Take enough rest for the next day.
