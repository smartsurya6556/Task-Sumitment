Swiggy Delivery Partner

Swiggy is one of India's leading online food delivery platforms. It connects customers with restaurants through a network of delivery partners. Delivery partners collect food from restaurants and deliver it safely and on time. Swiggy allows partners to work with flexible schedules, either part-time or full-time.

Incentives

Incentives are extra rewards that Swiggy may offer to delivery partners for completing eligible deliveries or meeting performance targets. Incentive programs can vary depending on the city, time, and current company policies.


Benefits of Incentives

  • 💵 Extra earnings for eligible deliveries.
  • 🍽️ Additional earning opportunities during busy meal times.
  • 🎯 Performance-based rewards.
  • ⭐ Rewards for maintaining good service quality.
  • 🎉 Special festival or promotional incentives may be available.

Flexible Working Hours

  • 🌞 Morning Shift
  • ☀️ Afternoon Shift
  • 🌆 Evening Shift
  • 🌙 Night Shift
  • ⏳ Part-Time
  • 🕒 Full-Time

Peak Hours

During these times, more customers usually place food orders

  • 🌅 Breakfast: 6:00 AM – 11:00 AM
  • 🍛 Lunch: 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • 🌇 Evening Snacks: 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM
  • 🍽️ Dinner: 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Zomato Delivery Partner

Zomato is one of India's leading food delivery platforms. Delivery partners collect food from restaurants and deliver it safely to customers.

Why Join Zomato?

  • Flexible working hours
  • Weekly earnings
  • Performance incentives
  • Easy registration process
  • Work near your location

Registration Process

  1. Download the Zomato Partner App.
  2. Register with your mobile number.
  3. Upload your documents.
  4. Complete verification.
  5. Start delivering orders.

Required Documents

Document Purpose
Aadhaar Card Identity Proof
PAN Card Verification
Driving Licence Driving Permission
Bank Account Salary Payment

Delivery Partner Responsibilities

  • Accept delivery orders.
  • Reach the restaurant.
  • Collect the food.
  • Deliver safely.
  • Maintain good customer ratings.

Rapido Captain

Rapido is a bike taxi and parcel delivery platform. Partners help customers travel safely and deliver small packages quickly.

Captain Responsibilities

  • Accept ride requests.
  • Reach the pickup location.
  • Confirm customer details.
  • Drive safely.
  • Complete the trip.
