Dear Delivery Partner

Deliver Fast, Earn More

Welcome to the Delivery Partner Guidance website. Learn how to become a delivery partner. Understand the delivery process. Follow safety rules. Improve your delivery skills.


What is a Delivery Partner?

  • Picks up orders from restaurants or stores.
  • Delivers food or packages to customers.
  • Uses a bike or scooter for delivery.
  • Works with delivery companies.
  • Provides fast and safe service.

Basic Requirements

  1. Minimum age: 18 years
  2. Valid driving licence
  3. Bike or scooter
  4. Smartphone with internet
  5. Aadhaar Card or ID Proof
  6. Bank account
  7. Good communication skills

How Delivery Works

  1. Login to the delivery app.
  2. Accept an order.
  3. Go to the restaurant.
  4. Pick up the order.
  5. Deliver to the customer.
  6. Confirm successful delivery.

Safety Tips

  • Wear a helmet.
  • Follow traffic rules.
  • Drive carefully.
  • Keep food safe.
  • Be polite to customers.
  • Check the delivery address.
  • Avoid using the phone while riding.


Benefits of Becoming a Delivery Partner

  1. Flexible working hours
  2. Daily earning opportunities
  3. Performance bonuses
  4. Incentive rewards
  5. Work near your location
  6. Meet new people
  7. Gain work experience
