Welcome to Tenkasi District

The Land of Waterfalls, Temples, Nature, and Rich Cultural Heritage


Tenkasi is one of the most beautiful districts in Tamil Nadu, famous for its breathtaking waterfalls, ancient temples, lush green Western Ghats, and pleasant climate. It is a perfect blend of natural beauty, history, spirituality, and agriculture, attracting thousands of tourists every year.

📖 District Introduction

Tenkasi District is located in the southern part of Tamil Nadu and was officially formed on 22 November 2019 by separating it from Tirunelveli District. The district headquarters is Tenkasi city. Known as the "Spa of South India," Tenkasi is famous for Courtallam Falls, scenic hills, fertile agricultural lands, and historic temples. Tourism, agriculture, trade, and small-scale industries play an important role in the district's economy. Tenkasi is also well connected by road and railway, making it an important destination for visitors and pilgrims.

🌊 Courtallam Falls

Courtallam Falls, also known as the "Spa of South India," is one of the most famous tourist destinations in Tenkasi District, Tamil Nadu. It is located at the foothills of the Western Ghats and is renowned for its scenic beauty and refreshing waterfalls. The water flows through forests rich in medicinal herbs, making it popular for its natural healing properties. Every year, especially during the monsoon season (June to September), thousands of tourists and pilgrims visit Courtallam to enjoy the waterfalls and pleasant climate. It is an important center for tourism, health, and nature lovers.

🛕 Kasi Viswanathar Temple

  • 🛕 One of the oldest Shiva temples in South Tamil Nadu.
  • 🏛️ Built in the beautiful Dravidian architectural style.
  • 🗼 Famous for its towering 180-foot Rajagopuram.
  • 🙏 Dedicated to Lord Shiva and attracts thousands of devotees every year.
  • 🎉 Celebrates grand festivals such as Maha Shivaratri and Navaratri.
  • 📸 A major tourist and pilgrimage destination in Tenkasi.

🌾 Agriculture in Tenkasi District


Agriculture is one of the most important occupations in Tenkasi District. The fertile land, rivers, and favorable climate help farmers grow a wide variety of crops throughout the year. Major crops include paddy, banana, coconut, sugarcane, and cotton. Agriculture provides employment and supports the livelihoods of many families in the district. It plays a vital role in the economic growth and food production of Tenkasi.
