Welcome to Tenkasi District

The Land of Waterfalls, Temples, Nature, and Rich Cultural Heritage


Tenkasi is one of the most beautiful districts in Tamil Nadu, famous for its breathtaking waterfalls, ancient temples, lush green Western Ghats, and pleasant climate. It is a perfect blend of natural beauty, history, spirituality, and agriculture, attracting thousands of tourists every year.

Male Population of Tenkasi District

The estimated male population of Tenkasi District is approximately 6.9 lakh to 7.0 lakh people. Men play a significant role in the district's economy by working in agriculture, government services, private industries, education, transportation, construction, retail businesses, and tourism.


Many young men from Tenkasi also pursue higher education and work in cities across Tamil Nadu and other states. Skilled workers, entrepreneurs, and professionals from the district contribute to the development of information technology, engineering, healthcare, and business sectors.

Female Population of Tenkasi District

The estimated female population of Tenkasi District is approximately 7.0 lakh to 7.1 lakh people. Women contribute significantly to family welfare, education, agriculture, healthcare, self-help groups, and entrepreneurship.

The literacy rate among women has improved considerably over the years due to better educational opportunities and government welfare programs. Many women work as teachers, nurses, government employees, entrepreneurs, farmers, and business owners.

Population Growth and Future Development

Tenkasi District has experienced gradual population growth due to improved healthcare, education, sanitation, and employment opportunities. Government welfare schemes, road connectivity, digital services, and tourism development have contributed to improving the quality of life.


As the district continues to develop, investments in education, healthcare, sustainable agriculture, and employment generation are expected to support balanced population growth while preserving the district's rich cultural heritage and natural environment.


📈 Development Highlights

  • 🏫 More schools and colleges.
  • 🏥 Better hospitals and healthcare facilities.
  • 🛣️ Improved roads and transport.
  • 🌐 Expansion of digital and internet services.
  • 💼 Growth in employment opportunities.
  • 🌊 Tourism development around Courtallam and nearby attractions.
  • 🌱 Sustainable agricultural practices and rural development.
