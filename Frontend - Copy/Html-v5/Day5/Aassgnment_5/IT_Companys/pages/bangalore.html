Bangalore

Bangalore IT Industry: Bangalore, officially known as Bengaluru, is called the Silicon Valley of India because it is the country's largest technology hub. The city is home to thousands of IT companies, startups, research centers, and multinational corporations. Bangalore specializes in software development, artificial intelligence (AI), cloud computing, cybersecurity, and product engineering. It has world-class IT parks such as Electronic City, Whitefield, Manyata Tech Park, and Bagmane Tech Park. Bangalore contributes a significant share of India's IT exports and provides employment to millions of technology professionals.
