Welcome to ABC College of Education

ABC College of Education was inaugurated in the year 2016 by his Excellency the then State Home Minister (Govt. of India), Shri Nityanand Rai. It is a premier institution, run and managed by Arcade Educational Trust, affiliated to Patliputra University and recognized by the NCTE & AICTE. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards in teacher education. Our journey began with the first session of B.Ed. programme in 2017 marking the start of our mission to empower future educators, we strive to create a generation of skilled, compassionate, and innovative teaching professionals equipped to meet the dynamic challenges of the modern educational landscape. And since then, we have grown into a hub for quality education and maintaining reputation for excellence, offering diverse professional courses namely B.Ed., B.Com. BBA, BCA, MCA and MBA. We believe that education is the cornerstone of progress and development. Our college is built on the pillars of academic excellence, professional ethics, and a commitment to fostering an inclusive and supportive learning environment. It is located in the vibrant city of Patna, near Danapur & Neora Railway station. We provide state-of-the-art facilities and access to a wide range of resources including Smart classes, projectors, library, laboratory, recreational facilities and experienced faculty to ensure our aspiring teacher educators receive a holistic education which integrates modern pedagogical practices, technology in education and value-based learning. Beyond academics, we encourage active participation in co-curricular activities, workshops, seminars, guest lectures and outreach programs to cultivate well-rounded individuals. Join ABC College of Education, Patna, and be part of a legacy that shapes the educators of tomorrow while contributing to the betterment of society. Together, let us create a brighter, more educated future!
